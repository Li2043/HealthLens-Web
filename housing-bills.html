Renting basics

Private renting in the UK usually involves a tenancy agreement, rent payments and shared responsibilities between tenants and landlords. Reading your contract carefully and keeping records of communications can help if problems arise.

Official and expert sources explain common terms, notice periods and tenant rights in plain English.

Deposits

Deposits must usually be protected in an approved scheme. Understanding how deposits work — and what deductions are allowed — can help you avoid disputes when you move out.

Read Citizens Advice housing guidance (opens in new tab)

Repairs, damp and mould

Landlords are generally responsible for keeping the property safe and in repair. Report problems in writing and keep a record. Damp and mould can affect health — seek advice if your landlord does not act.

Read Shelter student housing advice (opens in new tab)

Bills and shared housing

Shared houses often split costs for energy, water, internet and council tax (where applicable). Agree how bills are divided before signing, and check whether utilities are included in your rent.

See the Money & Spending page for budgeting guidance.

Where to get advice

Bristol SU Housing Advice offers independent support for University of Bristol students. Citizens Advice and Shelter provide expert housing guidance for wider questions.
