Student groups and societies

Bristol SU hosts hundreds of student groups — from academic societies to cultural networks and hobby clubs. Joining a group can be a practical way to meet people and feel part of campus life.

Browse Bristol SU groups and societies (opens in new tab)

Volunteering and community

Volunteering can help you connect with the local community while building skills and experience. Bristol SU Community Impact lists opportunities designed for students.

Explore volunteering opportunities (opens in new tab)

Sports and active wellbeing

The University of Bristol Sport centre offers clubs, classes and facilities for students at different levels — from beginners to competitive athletes.

Visit University of Bristol Sport (opens in new tab)

Low-cost activities

Many student groups and SU events are free or low-cost. Parks, libraries, campus events and society socials can be affordable ways to explore Bristol and meet people without overspending.

See the Money & Spending page for budgeting guidance.

Making connections as an international student

Building a social network in a new country takes time. Cultural societies, language exchanges, sport clubs and volunteering can all help. It is normal to feel lonely at first — see the Mental Wellbeing page for support signposting.
