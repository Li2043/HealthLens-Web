Need urgent help?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent support that is not life-threatening, contact NHS 111 or visit your nearest A&E.

How we choose sources

Source policy

HealthLens prioritises official and expert sources. Official sources include NHS, GOV.UK, universities and students' unions. Expert sources include organisations that specialise in student, housing, money, wellbeing or international student advice.

  • Official sources: rules, services and urgent support
  • Expert sources: practical explanations and specialist guidance
  • Student-facing resources: context for university life
