Care Options Guide

The Care Options Guide explains common UK healthcare support routes. It does not assess symptoms or decide where you should go. If you are unsure and need medical help now, use NHS 111. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E.

Pharmacy First: common conditions

NHS Pharmacy First may allow pharmacists to support some common conditions where clinically appropriate. Suitability can depend on age, symptoms and clinical judgement. A pharmacist can advise, treat where appropriate, or refer to a GP or another healthcare professional.

  • impetigo
  • infected insect bites
  • earache
  • sore throat
  • sinusitis
  • urinary tract infections
  • shingles

This list is for signposting only. It does not mean a pharmacy is always the right option. If you are unsure, use NHS 111 or contact a healthcare professional.

How we choose sources

Source policy

HealthLens prioritises official and expert sources. Official sources include NHS, GOV.UK, universities and students' unions. Expert sources include organisations that specialise in student, housing, money, wellbeing or international student advice. Each resource is summarised in plain English to help students understand what it is useful for.

  • Official sources: rules, services and urgent support
  • Expert sources: practical explanations and specialist guidance
  • Student-facing resources: context for university life
  • User responsibility: always check the original source for the latest information

HealthLens is a student project. It does not replace professional medical, legal, financial, immigration or emergency advice.
