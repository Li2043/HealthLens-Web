About HealthLens

A student project that helps international students in the UK navigate trusted support resources through clear topic hubs and a searchable resource library.

What HealthLens is

HealthLens is an International Student Wellbeing Navigator. It organises official and expert resources across study, wellbeing, healthcare, housing, money, community and everyday life — with plain-English summaries, source labels and suggested next steps.

It is designed to reduce confusion for students who are new to UK university systems, not to replace professional services or official websites.

Who it is for

HealthLens is primarily for international students studying in the UK, especially those who want a clearer way to find trusted resources across multiple areas of student life at once.

Many resources signposted on this demo use University of Bristol and Bristol SU examples, but the topic structure applies to broader UK student life themes.

What it does not do

  • It does not provide medical advice.
  • It does not provide legal, financial or immigration advice.
  • It does not assess symptoms or recommend treatment.
  • It does not replace official sources or emergency services.
  • It is not a deals, shopping or affiliate website.

Source policy

HealthLens prioritises official and expert sources. Each resource, health tip and search result includes source labels and a last checked date to help you understand what kind of information you are viewing.

How source labels work

HealthLens uses source labels to help users understand what kind of resource they are viewing.

Official source
Public institutions, universities, students’ unions and official health or government services. These are prioritised for rules, services, urgent support and eligibility information.
Expert source
Specialist organisations such as UKCISA, Citizens Advice, Shelter, MoneyHelper or mental health charities. These are used for practical explanations and subject-specific guidance.
Student-facing resource
Resources written for students that may provide useful context or lived-experience framing. These do not replace official or expert sources.
Commercial platform
Platforms that may provide discounts, shopping tools or services. These are included only when relevant to student life and are not treated as financial advice or recommendations.
Project note
HealthLens-created summaries or health tip cards. These are source-based editorial summaries and should be checked against the linked original sources.

What “Last checked” means

“Last checked” means the date this student project last checked that the link worked and that the summary still matched the source. It does not mean the original source was updated on that date.

Content limitations

HealthLens does not provide medical, legal, financial, immigration or emergency advice. For decisions that affect your health, housing, money, immigration status or safety, check the original source and contact the relevant professional or official service.

Content maintenance

This is a student project. The following is a proposed maintenance policy for portfolio demos — not a service-level promise:

  • Urgent and healthcare links: monthly review suggested
  • Visa, housing and money links: every 3 months suggested
  • Community and student activity links: every 6 months suggested
  • Project notes and health tip summaries: before each demo or application

HealthLens is a student project. It does not replace professional medical, legal, financial, immigration or emergency advice. Users should always check the original source for the latest information, eligibility rules, opening hours and contact details.

Accessibility approach

HealthLens follows WCAG-informed practices: semantic HTML, logical heading hierarchy, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive link text, form labels, live regions for search results, and reduced-motion support. The project is WCAG-informed, but not formally certified.

Testing is documented in accessibility-checklist.md, accessibility-test-report.md, audit report, docs/accessibility-audit.md and docs/manual-test-plan.md.

Technical overview

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript — static multi-page site, no frameworks
  • JSON content — `data/resources.json`, `data/care-options.json`, `data/articles.json` and `data/topics.json`
  • GitHub Pages — relative paths for deployment
  • Responsive layout — mobile, tablet and desktop breakpoints
  • Dark mode — manual toggle with `localStorage` preference
  • Print CSS — offline reference for key content
